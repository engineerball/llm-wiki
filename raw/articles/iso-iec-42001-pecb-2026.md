# ISO/IEC 42001 Artificial Intelligence Management System - PECB overview

**Publisher:** PECB
**Source URL:** https://pecb.com/en/education-and-certification-for-individuals/iso-iec-42001
**Retrieved:** 2026-09-10
**Related standard:** ISO/IEC 42001:2023, Information technology - Artificial intelligence - Management system

## Evidence Summary

PECB describes ISO/IEC 42001 as specifying requirements and guidance to establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS) within an organization.

PECB presents the AIMS as a framework for responsible AI implementation and associates it with fairness, transparency, accountability, and privacy.

PECB's training pathway includes ISO/IEC 42001 Foundation, ISO/IEC 42001 Lead Implementer, and ISO/IEC 42001 Lead Auditor.

PECB describes relevant competencies as understanding regulatory and ethical considerations, aligning AI initiatives with business objectives, supporting AIMS implementation, evaluating AI quality and reliability, and identifying and mitigating AI-related risks.

## Interpretation Boundary

PECB's page is training and certification guidance.
It is not the full text of ISO/IEC 42001 and should not be treated as a complete list of the standard's clauses or controls.
A PECB individual certificate is evidence of a person's training or certification status; it is not by itself an organizational ISO/IEC 42001 certification.
