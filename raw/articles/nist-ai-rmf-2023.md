# NIST AI Risk Management Framework

**Publisher:** National Institute of Standards and Technology
**Source URL:** https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
**Retrieved:** 2026-09-10

## Evidence Summary

NIST states that the AI Risk Management Framework is intended for voluntary use and helps organizations incorporate trustworthiness considerations into the design, development, use, and evaluation of AI products, services, and systems.

NIST released AI RMF 1.0 on 2023-01-26.
NIST also provides an AI RMF Playbook, Roadmap, Crosswalk, and Trustworthy and Responsible AI Resource Center.

NIST released NIST AI 600-1, the Generative AI Profile, on 2024-07-26.
The profile helps organizations identify risks specific to generative AI and propose risk-management actions aligned with organizational goals and priorities.

## Interpretation Boundary

AI RMF is a voluntary risk-management framework, not an ISO certification standard and not a substitute for applicable law.
Organizations may use it as an implementation and control-design reference alongside an AIMS and sector or jurisdictional obligations.
