# Software Ecology

Lens for studying software engineering as a holistic sociotechnical ecosystem — people and technology together — rather than focusing on code artifacts in isolation.

Coined/used by [[Adam Bender]] at Google I/O. The framing shifts attention from individual trees (lines of code, microservices) to the forest (the ecosystem as a whole).

## Why It Matters

Traditional engineering analysis focuses on technical artifacts. Software ecology includes:
- Team dynamics and knowledge distribution
- Review and deployment pipelines
- Token and compute economics
- Cultural mentorship patterns

## Practical Application

Use [[Systems Thinking]] (Why? / What if? questioning) to analyze the ecosystem rather than individual components.

The [[Architecture Diagram Test]] is a concrete tool for measuring ecosystem health.
