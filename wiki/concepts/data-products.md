# Data Products

A "model-first" approach to data management where data is treated as a product with defined purpose, quality standards (SLOs), and access controls.

## Semantic Context
In the context of the [[semantic-layer]] movement, data products provide the dependable foundation required to avoid [[semantic-mistrust]].

### Model-First Approach
As advocated by [[animesh-kumar]], a model-first data product:
1. Starts with the **context** (logical model).
2. Defines requirements and SLOs.
3. Moves down to **activation** in the physical layer.

## Integration
Data products are the first layer of the [[reliable-semantic-stack]], ensuring that the semantics exposed to users and LLMs are grounded in governed and reliable data.
