# Generative AI

AI systems that generate novel content — text, code, images, audio — by learning patterns from training data. Distinguished from discriminative AI (which classifies) by its ability to produce new outputs rather than just predict labels.

## Relevance to This Wiki

- **Semantic layers** are increasingly required to ground generative AI in accurate, governed business data — without them, LLMs produce "dangerously plausible" but inconsistent outputs
- **[[graph-rag|Graph-RAG]]** extends retrieval-augmented generation with explicit relationship graphs, improving precision for generative AI applications
- **Agent frameworks** (MCP, A2A, harness engineering) exist to make generative AI reliable and controllable in production workflows
