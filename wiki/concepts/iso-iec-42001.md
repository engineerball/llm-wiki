# ISO/IEC 42001

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 is the international management-system standard for an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS).
It specifies requirements and provides guidance for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an AIMS within an organization.

## What an AIMS Means

An AIMS is an organizational management system for directing AI-related objectives, responsibilities, risk treatment, operational controls, monitoring, review, and continual improvement.
It is not a model-quality score, a product safety label, or a guarantee that every AI output is correct.

The management-system approach is useful because AI risk changes when the model, data, users, use case, provider, application workflow, or jurisdiction changes.
The organization needs repeatable governance processes rather than a one-time technical review.

## PECB Relationship

PECB offers training and certification pathways including:

- ISO/IEC 42001 Foundation
- ISO/IEC 42001 Lead Implementer
- ISO/IEC 42001 Lead Auditor

PECB describes relevant capabilities as understanding regulatory and ethical considerations, aligning AI initiatives with business objectives, implementing an AIMS, evaluating AI quality and reliability, and mitigating AI-related risks.

An individual PECB credential demonstrates a person's training or certification status.
It does not by itself certify an organization's AIMS.
Organizational certification requires an applicable conformity-assessment process by a competent certification body.

## Practical Adoption

Organizations can use ISO/IEC 42001 as the top-level management-system structure and connect it to:

- AI inventory and scope
- risk assessment and treatment
- lifecycle and change management
- data governance
- security and privacy controls
- human oversight
- supplier and model-provider management
- monitoring, incidents, corrective action, and management review
- internal audit and continual improvement

The full ISO standard should be consulted for authoritative requirements.
Public PECB summaries are useful orientation material but do not replace the standard text or certification-body guidance.
