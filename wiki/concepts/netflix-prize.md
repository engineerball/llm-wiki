# Netflix Prize

A 2006 competition by Netflix offering a \$1M prize for improving collaborative filtering accuracy by 10%. Sparked major advances in [[matrix-factorization|matrix factorization]] for recommendation systems.

Researchers learned to decompose the user-item interaction matrix into low-dimensional [[embeddings|embeddings]] (originally called "latent categories"), pioneering the embedding approach that later became central to [[two-tower-architecture|two tower models]].
