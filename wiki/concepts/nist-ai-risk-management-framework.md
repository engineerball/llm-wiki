# NIST AI Risk Management Framework

The NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) is a voluntary framework intended to help organizations incorporate trustworthiness considerations into the design, development, use, and evaluation of AI systems.

NIST released AI RMF 1.0 on 2023-01-26 and later published a Generative AI Profile, NIST AI 600-1, on 2024-07-26.

## How to Use It

AI RMF can serve as an operational risk-management reference inside a broader AI governance program or AIMS.
It can help teams structure risk discovery, control selection, evaluation, monitoring, and communication without presenting itself as an ISO certification scheme.

The framework is especially useful for creating a common vocabulary between executives, product teams, engineers, legal, privacy, security, and audit.

## Generative AI

The Generative AI Profile helps organizations identify risks that are distinctive or intensified in generative AI, then select risk-management actions aligned with organizational goals and priorities.
Relevant implementation areas include misuse, privacy, information integrity, harmful content, data and model supply chains, evaluation, monitoring, and human oversight.

## Boundary

NIST AI RMF is voluntary guidance.
It does not replace applicable law, contractual duties, sector controls, privacy obligations, or an organization’s management-system certification requirements.
