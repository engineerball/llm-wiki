# Adam Bender

Software engineer at Google. Speaker at Google I/O on AI's second-order impacts on software engineering practices.

Known for the concept of [[Software Ecology]] — analyzing the sociotechnical ecosystems that produce software rather than focusing on individual code artifacts.

## Key Ideas

- [[AI 10x Moment]]: coding velocity explosion that doesn't automatically translate to engineering quality
- AI as amplifier: direction matters more than magnitude
- Four architectural pillars for AI-first development (see [[Software Engineering at the Tipping Point]])
- [[Architecture Diagram Test]] as diagnostic for organizational intellectual control
