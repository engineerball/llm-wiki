# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and software engineer. Former Director of AI at Tesla, former Senior Director of AI at OpenAI. Known for contributions to deep learning, especially computer vision and large language models.

Creator of the **llm-wiki pattern** — a design for building personal knowledge bases where an LLM incrementally maintains a persistent wiki rather than relying on retrieval-based RAG.

Former Stanford PhD student under Fei-Fei Li. Creator of karpathy/nn and other influential deep learning projects. Known for clear, accessible explanations of complex ML concepts.
