# Animesh Kumar

Data strategist and author; contributor to [[modern-data-101]]. Focuses on the evolution of the [[semantic-layer]], [[data-products]], and reliable data architectures.

## Key Ideas
- **Semantic Movement:** The shift towards semantics as a foundational, decoupled layer in the modern data stack.
- **[[reliable-semantic-stack]]:** A three-layer model for semantic success (Data Products, Catalog, Application Layer).
- **[[semantic-mistrust]]:** The risk of delegitimizing the semantic layer by building it on poor-quality physical data.
