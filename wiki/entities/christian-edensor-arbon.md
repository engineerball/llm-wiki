# Christian Edensor Arbon

Data engineering practitioner and writer. Author of the "Building a Semantic Layer" article series on Medium (Towards Data Engineering publication). Building a personal simplified semantic layer inspired by [[Airbnb Minerva]] 2.0. Focused on distilling enterprise-scale data architecture patterns (Airbnb, Databricks) into implementations accessible to small teams without large engineering budgets.
