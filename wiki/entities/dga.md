# DGA — Digital Government Development Agency (Thailand)

**Thai name:** สำนักงานพัฒนารัฐบาลดิจิทัล (องค์การมหาชน) — สพร.  
**Type:** Public Organisation under the Prime Minister's Office  
**Address:** 999, 4th Floor, Thailand Institute of Justice, Chaengwattana Road, Thung Song Hong, Laksi, Bangkok 10210  
**Website:** www.dga.or.th  
**Cloud policy portal:** https://kb.dga.or.th/cloud/

## Role

DGA is the implementing authority for Thailand's digital government standards and the **"Go Cloud First" (นโยบายการใช้คลาวด์เป็นหลัก)** policy. It operates under the Digital Government Development Committee (คณะกรรมการพัฒนารัฐบาลดิจิทัล).

Key responsibilities under the Cloud First Policy:
- Publish **Digital Government Standards (DGS / มรด.)** covering cloud usage guidelines, data classification, and CSP certification standards
- Operate the **Cloud Budget Request Portal** (https://kb.dga.or.th/cloud/) through which agencies submit cloud projects for national budget allocation
- Review exception cases (e.g. Private Cloud requests for non-highly-protected data)
- Govern the CSP Tier certification ecosystem

## Cloud First Policy Context

Cabinet announcement on 11 September 2023 established "Go Cloud First" as a national policy. On 25 June 2024, the Cabinet formally established a dedicated Cloud First Policy Committee and assigned DGA as the operational lead.

**Four components of the Cloud First framework:**
1. Demand management (หน่วยงานต้องการใช้คลาวด์)
2. Supply management (ผู้ให้บริการคลาวด์)
3. Government Cloud Management (การบริหารจัดการ)
4. Cloud Service Ecosystem (ระบบนิเวศ connecting demand and supply)
