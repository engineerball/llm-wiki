# Harley-Davidson

American motorcycle manufacturer. Used by [[Simon Sinek]] as an example of a brand whose logo became a tattoo — the ultimate loyalty signal.

## Why People Tattoo the Logo

Harley-Davidson has communicated its beliefs consistently over decades: freedom, individuality, rebellion against ordinary life. Customers don't just buy a motorcycle; they join a belief system.

The logo tattoo proves [[Purpose-Driven Leadership]] at its extreme: the brand is not a product identifier — it's a personal identity marker. Some fans tattoo it without even owning a Harley.

## Key Principle Illustrated

Any symbol — logo, gesture, icon — can become meaningful only when backed by clear, consistent WHY. Without stated purpose, a logo is just a picture.
