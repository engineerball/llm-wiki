# Linux Foundation

Non-profit organization that hosts and supports open-source projects through shared infrastructure, collaborative development, and neutral governance.

## Relevant to This Wiki

Governs several agentic AI protocols and infrastructure:

| Project | Role |
|---|---|
| MCP (Model Context Protocol) | Governing body (Apache 2.0) |
| A2A (Agent-to-Agent) | Governing body (Apache 2.0) |
| [[agentgateway]] | Hosting organization |

MCP: originally launched by [[anthropic]] Nov 2024, transferred to Linux Foundation governance. Now adopted by [[openai]], [[google]], [[microsoft]].

A2A: [[google]]-originated protocol for inter-agent coordination.
