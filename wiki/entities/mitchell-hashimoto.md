# Mitchell Hashimoto

Co-founder of HashiCorp (Terraform, Vault, Nomad). Credited with introducing the term "Harness Engineering" in the context of AI coding agents.

## Relevance

- Coined "Harness Engineering" — the practice of designing scaffolding that makes AI agents reliable, predictable, and safe to operate
- Referenced by [[trae]] and [[birgitta-bockeler]] as the origin of the term
