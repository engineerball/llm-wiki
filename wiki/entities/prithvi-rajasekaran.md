# Prithvi Rajasekaran

Engineer at [[anthropic]]. Author of *Harness Design for Long-Running Application Development* (anthropic.com, 2026).

## Contributions to Harness Engineering

- Described the [[generator-evaluator-architecture]] pattern for long-running agent tasks
- Introduced [[sprint-contracts]] as the negotiation mechanism between generator and evaluator agents
- Documented [[context-management-for-agents]]: context anxiety in Claude Sonnet 4.5, context resets vs compaction
- Central thesis: "Every component in a harness encodes an assumption about what the model can't do on its own, and those assumptions are worth stress testing."
