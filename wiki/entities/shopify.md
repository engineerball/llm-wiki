# Shopify

- **Tags:** organization, e-commerce, platform
- **Sources:** [[wiki/sources/shopify-universal-commerce-protocol-ucp-2026.md]]

## Overview

Shopify is a global e-commerce platform serving millions of merchants worldwide. After 20+ years and billions of transactions, Shopify co-developed the **Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP)** with Google to create an open standard for AI agent commerce.

## Key Contributions

- **Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP):** Co-developed with Google, UCP enables AI agents to discover, negotiate, and transact with any merchant
- **Checkout Kit:** Originally built embedded checkout at scale; distilled into the open ECP protocol
- **PCIv4 Compliance:** Provides strong sandboxing for agent-merchant payment compliance

## UCP Design Principles (Shopify's Vision)

- Discovery and negotiation over rigid integration
- Layered extensibility (capabilities + extensions)
- Graceful handoff for human-in-the-loop transactions
- Payment flexibility — two-sided negotiation between merchant and buyer
- Open bazaar model — no committees, no gatekeepers
