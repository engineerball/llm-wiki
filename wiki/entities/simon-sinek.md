# Simon Sinek

Author and organizational consultant. Best known for *Start With Why* (2009) and the [[Golden Circle]] framework. TED Talk "How Great Leaders Inspire Action" is one of the most-watched of all time.

## Core Ideas

- [[Golden Circle]]: WHY → HOW → WHAT; great leaders communicate inside-out
- [[Manipulation vs Inspiration]]: two paths to changing behavior; only inspiration builds lasting loyalty
- [[Purpose-Driven Leadership]]: purpose precedes strategy; companies that lose their WHY lose their identity
- [[Diffusion of Innovation]]: win early adopters who share beliefs, not the majority

## Frameworks

- [[Golden Circle]]: WHY → HOW → WHAT rings; inside-out communication
- **CDC**: Clarity of WHY, Discipline of HOW, Consistency of WHAT — three operating conditions for Golden Circle execution
- [[Celery Test]]: diagnostic for WHY clarity — if decision is not instant, WHY is not clear enough
- [[Why-HOW Split]]: every WHY visionary needs a HOW operator to build durable structure
- [[Diffusion of Innovation]]: win Innovators + Early Adopters (combined 16%) to trigger tipping point
- [[Manipulation vs Inspiration]]: only two paths to behavior change; only one builds loyalty

## Key Examples Used

- [[Apple]] — archetypal WHY-first company; empowers individuals to challenge status quo
- [[Harley-Davidson]] — logo tattooed by fans because beliefs are clear and consistent
- [[Walmart]] — cautionary tale; lost WHY after Sam Walton's death
- Martin Luther King Jr. — inspired nation-scale change without any new ideas
- Wright Brothers — won the airplane race despite having less funding; [[Samuel Pierpont Langley]] contrast (WHAT-driven, quit when they won)
- [[Southwest Airlines]] — CDC framework in action; WHY: champion of ordinary people
- [[Walt Disney]] + Roy Disney — WHY-HOW split; visionary + operator pair
- [[Steve Jobs]] + Apple — WHY-first; ouster proved WHY is identity not strategy
