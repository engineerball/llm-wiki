# Southwest Airlines

American low-cost carrier. Used by [[Simon Sinek]] as a primary example of the CDC framework (Clarity, Discipline, Consistency) in action.

## WHY-First Model

Founded ~1971 with a clear WHY: *be the champion of ordinary people* who couldn't afford to fly in 1970s America.

- **WHY**: democratize air travel; ordinary people deserve to fly too
- **HOW**: low costs, fun culture, no-frills simplicity
- **WHAT**: cheap domestic flights with famously cheerful staff

This sequence is the [[Golden Circle]] executed perfectly. Southwest didn't start with "let's do cheap flights" — they started with who they were for.

## Competitive Durability

Many low-cost, "fun" airline competitors emerged after Southwest. None displaced them as category leader. Price-matching is easy. Copying a genuine WHY is not. Competitors replicated the WHAT and HOW without the WHY, and customers sensed the difference.
