# Tiago Forte

Productivity author and knowledge management consultant. Creator of the [[para-method|PARA Method]] and author of *Building a Second Brain* (2022, Atria Books).

## Contributions Relevant to This Wiki

### PARA Method

Forte's PARA taxonomy (Projects, Areas, Resources, Archives) was originally designed for human knowledge management. It was later adapted by [[meta|Meta]]'s analytics team as the **context architecture** for their AI agent harness — the folder structure tells the agent what's active, what's important, and where to route new information.

See [[para-method]] for the full concept page and its relationship to [[progressive-disclosure]] and [[harness-engineering]].
