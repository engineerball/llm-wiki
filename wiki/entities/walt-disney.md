# Walt Disney

Co-founder of The Walt Disney Company with his brother Roy Disney. Used by [[Simon Sinek]] to illustrate the WHY-HOW visionary-operator split — a structural requirement for scaling purpose-driven organizations.

## WHY Visionary

Walt's WHY: *bring happiness and magic to people through storytelling.* He was the dreamer, the creative source, the originator of belief. Impractical as a solo operator.

## Roy Disney — The HOW Operator

Walt freely admitted he could not have built the company without Roy, who served as HOW executor: financial discipline, operational structure, business strategy.

Walt generated the ideas and vision. Roy built the systems that made those ideas real at scale. Neither could have created The Walt Disney Company alone.

## The Pattern

This WHY-visionary + HOW-operator pairing appears repeatedly in history:
- Walt Disney (WHY) + Roy Disney (HOW)
- Steve Jobs (WHY) + Steve Wozniak (HOW)
- Martin Luther King Jr. (WHY) + organizational infrastructure (HOW)

The visionary without an operator generates inspiration but no durable structure. The operator without a visionary builds efficient machines with no soul.
