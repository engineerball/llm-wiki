# AI Governance Best Practices: How to Build Responsible and Effective AI Programs

**Source:** Databricks Blog
**URL:** https://www.databricks.com/blog/ai-governance-best-practices-how-build-responsible-and-effective-ai-programs
**Publisher:** Databricks Staff
**Retrieved:** 2026-09-10

## Summary

Databricks presents enterprise AI governance as a cross-functional operating program for risk, compliance, trust, and responsible scaling.
The article stresses that governance covers data, prompts, models, workflows, human decisions, and downstream applications.

Its practical recommendations include clear ownership, risk-tiered controls, lifecycle checkpoints, standardized documentation, monitoring, incident response, human oversight for high-risk decisions, regulatory watch, and a centralized-federated operating model.

## Key Claims

- Inventory current AI systems and assign accountable owners.
- Define intended and prohibited use before development.
- Record data sources, ownership, consent constraints, and limitations.
- Establish evaluation metrics and acceptable trade-offs before testing.
- Require release gates and rollback criteria aligned with risk tier.
- Monitor performance, drift, unexpected inputs and outputs, and use outside approved scope.
- Maintain system summaries, data documentation, evaluation summaries, monitoring plans, approval records, and incident records.
- Use centralized standards with federated domain execution.

## Caveat

This is practical vendor guidance, not a normative standard or legal opinion.
