# EU AI Act and OECD AI Principles - governance references

**EU source:** https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai
**OECD source:** https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles
**Retrieved:** 2026-09-10

The European Commission describes the EU AI Act as a risk-based legal framework for AI providers and deployers.
Its high-risk obligations include risk assessment and mitigation, dataset quality, logging and traceability, technical documentation, information for deployers, human oversight, robustness, cybersecurity, and accuracy.

The OECD AI Principles provide a values-based reference for trustworthy AI, including inclusive growth and sustainable development, human rights and democratic values, transparency and explainability, robustness/security/safety, and accountability.

The EU AI Act is law with jurisdictional scope.
The OECD Principles are policy principles and are not a certification scheme.
