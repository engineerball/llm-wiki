# FinOps Personas

**Source:** FinOps Foundation — FinOps Framework
**URL:** <https://www.finops.org/framework/personas/>

## Overview

Implementing FinOps requires many **Personas** — broad stakeholder groups — to work collaboratively. Personas represent groups, not individual people. In large orgs, a Persona may reflect many people across roles; in small orgs, one person may cover multiple Personas.

The framework divides personas into two categories: **Core** (always involved) and **Allied** (may intersect).

## Core Personas

These are always involved in FinOps. Each has distinct perspectives, challenges, metrics, and outcomes.

### 1. Engineering
- **Role:** Architect, build, deploy, and operate cloud infrastructure and services
- **Perspective:** Want to ship features fast; costs are secondary concern
- **FinOps challenge:** Need cost as a first-class metric alongside performance and velocity
- **Outcomes sought:** Reliable deployments, optimal architecture, resource efficiency

### 2. Finance
- **Role:** Budget planning, forecasting, financial reporting, cost allocation
- **Perspective:** Need predictability, accountability, ROI visibility
- **FinOps challenge:** Cloud is variable/opex; traditional budget models don't fit well
- **Outcomes sought:** Accurate cost allocation, budget adherence, financial transparency

### 3. Procurement
- **Role:** Vendor contracts, rate negotiations, commitment purchases, license management
- **Perspective:** Want to maximize rate optimization and contract value
- **FinOps challenge:** Cloud pricing is complex, dynamic, requires technical understanding
- **Outcomes sought:** Best pricing terms, optimal commitment levels, contract compliance

### 4. IT Operations
- **Role:** Day-to-day cloud infrastructure management, service reliability, capacity planning
- **Perspective:** Focus on uptime, performance, and operational efficiency
- **FinOps challenge:** Balancing reliability/cost trade-offs; rightsizing without risk
- **Outcomes sought:** Efficient capacity, stable operations, predictable costs

### 5. Product / Business
- **Role:** Define product strategy, set priorities, manage business P&L
- **Perspective:** Want technology spend to map to measurable business value
- **FinOps challenge:** Connecting cloud costs to unit economics and customer value
- **Outcomes sought:** Profitable products, clear cost-per-customer, value-driven decisions

### 6. Executive / Cloud Center of Excellence (CCoE)
- **Role:** CTO/CFO set strategic direction, define cloud adoption vision, allocate budget
- **Perspective:** High-level ROI, competitive advantage, cost efficiency at scale
- **FinOps challenge:** Translating FinOps outcomes to business impact and competitive positioning
- **Outcomes sought:** Strategic cloud ROI, organizational efficiency, cost governance

## Allied Personas

These roles are not directly part of FinOps but intersect with it through the **Intersecting Disciplines** framework capability.

### ITAM — IT Asset Management
Collaborate with FinOps, Finance, and Procurement to achieve efficiency, transparency, and value in managing IT assets.

**Intersecting responsibilities:**
- Asset discovery and inventory
- Asset auditing and compliance
- License management
- Cost analysis and optimization
- Documentation and reporting
- Stakeholder collaboration

### ITFM — IT Financial Management
When operating separately from Finance, responsible for collecting, categorizing, and reporting IT spending. Ensures spending is clearly recorded, aligned with business priorities, and mapped to measurable value.

**Intersecting responsibilities:**
- Budgeting and forecasting support
- Cost accounting & optimization
- Financial analysis
- Investment prioritization
- Financial reporting
- Continuous process improvements
- Documentation and reporting
- Stakeholder collaboration

### Sustainability
Collaborate with FinOps to optimize technology usage for environmental impact, drive accountability, and accelerate progress toward sustainability goals.

**Intersecting responsibilities:**
- Optimization for sustainable initiatives
- Waste reduction focus
- Policy and compliance
- Efficiency and optimization analysis
- Documentation and reporting
- Stakeholder collaboration

### ITSM / ITIL
Collaborate with FinOps to standardize and streamline IT service operations, improve quality and reliability, while balancing service levels against cost management priorities.

**Intersecting responsibilities:**
- Service design and architecture
- Service operation & improvement
- Service level monitoring & management
- Change management
- Cost analysis and optimization
- Documentation and reporting
- Stakeholder collaboration

### Security (IT Security)
Collaborate with FinOps to partner on transparency of usage in both operational and security domains, optimize security spending, improve IT Security financial governance, and strengthen cloud security posture.

**Intersecting responsibilities:**
- Monitoring and anomaly response
- Anomaly investigation and analysis
- Policy and compliance
- Identity and access management
- Documentation and reporting
- Stakeholder collaboration

## Key Takeaways

- **Personas ≠ individuals** — they're broad stakeholder groups; one person can fill multiple roles in small orgs
- **Core vs Allied** — Core personas are always involved; Allied personas intersect as needed
- **Each Persona has different perspectives** — what matters to Engineering (speed) differs from Finance (predictability) — FinOps bridges these gaps
- **Intersecting Disciplines** — Allied personas map to the "Intersecting Disciplines" capability within the FinOps Framework
