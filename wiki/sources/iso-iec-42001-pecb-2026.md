# ISO/IEC 42001 Artificial Intelligence Management System - PECB overview

**Source:** PECB
**URL:** https://pecb.com/en/education-and-certification-for-individuals/iso-iec-42001
**Retrieved:** 2026-09-10

PECB describes ISO/IEC 42001 as specifying requirements and guidance for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an Artificial Intelligence Management System within an organization.

PECB associates the AIMS with responsible AI implementation and principles including fairness, transparency, accountability, and privacy.

The page lists Foundation, Lead Implementer, and Lead Auditor training and certification pathways.

## Boundary

This page is a PECB training and certification overview, not the full standard text.
An individual PECB certificate should not be confused with organizational ISO/IEC 42001 certification.
