# NIST AI Risk Management Framework

**Source:** National Institute of Standards and Technology
**URL:** https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
**Retrieved:** 2026-09-10

NIST describes AI RMF as a voluntary framework for incorporating trustworthiness into the design, development, use, and evaluation of AI systems.
AI RMF 1.0 was released on 2023-01-26.
NIST also provides a Playbook, Roadmap, Crosswalk, and Trustworthy and Responsible AI Resource Center.

The Generative AI Profile, NIST AI 600-1, was released on 2024-07-26 to help organizations identify and manage risks specific to generative AI.
