## Summary

Second Thai summary of Simon Sinek's *Start With Why* (2009), from panasm.com. Covers all 5 parts. Adds distinct content not in the [[start-with-why-thai-summary|Lucid Trader summary]]: CDC framework, Celery Test, Southwest Airlines example, Langley vs Wright Brothers contrast, WHY-HOW visionary-operator split (Walt/Roy Disney), Steve Jobs ouster from Apple, and metric drift as the mechanism of WHY decay.

## Key Claims

- WHY must be accompanied by CDC: Clarity of WHY, Discipline of HOW, Consistency of WHAT.
- The [[Celery Test]]: if your WHY is clear, you instantly know which decisions belong — no deliberation needed.
- [[Samuel Pierpont Langley]] had every advantage over the Wright Brothers (funding, fame, team) but was driven by WHAT (glory). He quit when they won. WHY-driven leaders don't quit when others succeed first.
- Every WHY visionary needs an HOW operator: Walt Disney (WHY) + Roy Disney (HOW); Steve Jobs (WHY) + Steve Wozniak (HOW).
- Apple lost its WHY when Steve Jobs was ousted. Jobs returned and restored it. WHY is not product strategy — it's identity.
- Metric drift: organizations decay from WHY to WHAT when they measure only WHAT (sales, stock price) with no WHY metrics.
- Innovators + Early Adopters = 16% combined. Reaching this threshold triggers the tipping point.
