## Summary

Thai summary of Simon Sinek's *Start With Why* (2009). Covers all 6 parts: why most organizations fail to inspire, the Golden Circle framework, the biology behind WHY-first communication, how to find followers, the challenge of scaling purpose, and competing against yourself.

## Key Claims

- Every organization knows WHAT they do. Few know WHY.
- Great leaders communicate inside-out: WHY → HOW → WHAT. Most do the opposite.
- Manipulation (price, promotions, fear) changes behavior short-term but builds zero loyalty.
- Inspiration from a clear WHY speaks to the limbic brain — the decision-making center — not the neocortex.
- You only need to win 15–18% early adopters who share your beliefs; the majority follows.
- Purpose decay is fatal: Walmart lost its WHY after Sam Walton died and became just a price competitor.
- Purpose is discovered by looking backward, not invented through market research.
- Charisma comes from clear purpose, not from power or energy.
- Company values posted on walls are nouns — useless. Rewrite as verbs (actions) to make them operational.
