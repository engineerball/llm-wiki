# Five Things That Differentiate Cloud Cost Management and FinOps

**Source:** ThoughtWorks Blog
**URL:** https://www.thoughtworks.com/en-us/insights/blog/cloud/5_cloud_cost_management_finops

## Overview

ThoughtWorks outlines five key differences between cloud cost management (narrow, reactive, siloed) and FinOps (broad, proactive, collaborative). Positions FinOps as a more mature and comprehensive approach that optimizes cloud spend while aligning with business goals.

## Five Differences

| # | Dimension | Cloud Cost Management | FinOps |
|---|-----------|----------------------|--------|
| 1 | **Approach** | Reactive — investigate spikes after they happen | Proactive — alerts before reaching thresholds |
| 2 | **Goals** | Reduce cloud spend by eliminating waste | Optimize spend to align with broader business goals |
| 3 | **Focus** | Technical elements: tagging, right-sizing, terminating unused resources | Technical + financial: budgeting, forecasting, and reporting alongside technical optimization |
| 4 | **Collaboration** | Siloed — single team (IT manages, finance approves) | Cross-functional — engineering, finance, operations collaborate |
| 5 | **Maturity** | Starting point for orgs new to cloud | Evolved discipline and cultural practice for continuous value maximization |

## Additional Distinctions

### Scope
- Cloud cost management = the "how" (methods for controlling spend)
- FinOps = the "why" and "what" (strategic rationale, business value)

### Breadth of Optimization
- Cloud cost optimization = narrowly focused on reducing expenses
- FinOps = broader: cost optimization + budgeting + forecasting + reporting

### Metrics
- Cloud cost management = financial metrics only (total spend, cost per unit)
- FinOps = financial + non-financial metrics (user satisfaction, business agility)

Example: A FinOps team might recommend investing in a more expensive cloud service that improves application performance — costs go up but user satisfaction and business agility improve.

### Organizational Difference
- Cloud cost management = siloed (IT manages, finance approves, teams work at cross-purposes)
- FinOps = all stakeholders involved (engineering, operations, finance) — identifies cost-saving measures aligned with business goals

## Key Cultural Difference

> "The key cultural difference: FinOps takes a collaborative approach making sure that everyone collectively owns cost management."
